WHY THIS PROJECT?

Fashion data is everywhere.

Modern fashion platforms contain thousands of products. Every product can have an image, a name and other descriptive information.

Manually categorizing these products can be time-consuming and inconsistent.

Our project explores how deep learning can combine visual information with language information to automatically understand and categorize fashion products.

🖼️
Understand the product image Identify visual patterns, shapes and appearance.
📝
Understand product language Extract meaning from product names.
🔗
Combine both modalities Fuse visual and semantic features.
🎯
Predict the category Classify the product into one of ten fashion categories.
WHAT WE BUILT

Two modalities.
One prediction.

The system processes the same product through two different deep learning pathways before combining their learned representations.

01 / VISION

MobileNetV2

A pretrained convolutional neural network used to extract visual features from product images.

02 / LANGUAGE

DistilBERT

A transformer-based language model used to create semantic representations of product names.

03 / FUSION

Feature Fusion

Visual and textual embeddings are concatenated into a combined representation.

04 / CLASSIFICATION

ANN

A neural network learns from the fused representation and predicts the product category.
